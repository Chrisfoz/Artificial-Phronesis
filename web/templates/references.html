Foundational Works

Key papers that define the core concepts in this knowledge graph:

Jeste, D. V., Lee, E. E., & Cassidy, C. (2020). Beyond artificial intelligence: exploring artificial wisdom.

International Psychogeriatrics, 32(8), 993-1001.

Proposes extending AI beyond performance intelligence toward wisdom capacities including emotional regulation, pro-social orientation, and ethical behavior.

Grossmann, I., & Johnson, S. (2025). Imagining and building wise machines.

Discusses operationalizing wisdom in AI systems, emphasizing metacognition, perspective-taking, and intellectual humility.

McGregor, S. (2025). The Philosophy of Artificial Wisdom.

Philosophical analysis of artificial wisdom vs. artificial phronesis, examining their relationship to AGI and consciousness.

Tsai, C. (n.d.). The Possibility of Artificial Phronesis.

Seven-stage framework for practical wisdom, analyzing whether machines can achieve phronetic excellence.

Schmidhuber, J., et al. (2024). Darwin Gödel Machines.

arXiv:2410.06087

Self-improving AI using proof search and population-based evolution—a step toward open-ended machine intelligence.

Lu, C., et al. (2024). The AI Scientist: Towards Fully Automated Open-Ended Scientific Discovery.

Automated research system demonstrating metacognitive capabilities through self-review and idea generation.
